How to interpret the result

Task delegation. Each attempt costs tokens. When it succeeds, the model values the human hours saved after direction and review. When it fails, a person completes the original task, plus any extra failure effort you enter. The success rate describes a group of attempts, so the result is an average.

Productivity uplift. A 2.83× multiplier means 2.83 times the baseline output during the same session. The result values the extra output at your hourly input and subtracts the costs you enter. The realization setting lets you adjust how much of that theoretical value the company can capture.

How well do the research presets transfer? METR’s 65% example is an approximate reading of its historical fit for an early Mythos Preview on well-specified technical tasks. OpenAI’s rates describe its own research tasks, with 3 and 6 hours used here as illustrative midpoints. These rates are starting points for scenarios, and your own task data can be different.

The 2.83× figure comes from a model of Anthropic research output and depends on assumptions about the value and quality of code. In a separate METR survey, technical workers reported a median 1.4–2× change in value of work. A 2025 controlled study found slower completion for experienced open-source developers on their own repositories with the tools then available. The methods, populations, and tools differ, so results should be interpreted in context. METR survey · METR controlled study
