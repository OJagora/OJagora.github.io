The blended-price view shows 40 model families, averaging scores and prices across listed settings. Prices are 3:1 input/output USD per million tokens. Only models with scores published for this index version are included; coverage of older releases may be limited. Source-estimated scores are marked in tooltips. Scores from different index versions are not directly comparable. The task-cost view shows 82 configurations with published benchmark costs, including actual token use, caching, and reasoning. Configurations without measured costs are omitted. Each year-end frontier includes models released by 31 December of its label year; “current” includes all shown releases. Frontiers use this dataset’s values, not archived historical prices or scores. The Pareto line connects models for which no selected model is both at least as capable and no more expensive, with at least one strict improvement. Release-date timeline starts at ChatGPT public release on 30 November 2022.
